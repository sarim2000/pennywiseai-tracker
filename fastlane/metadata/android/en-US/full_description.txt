Your bank already texts you every transaction. PennyWise reads those SMS on your phone and turns them into an automatic expense tracker — no manual entry, no sign-up, nothing uploaded.

★ Why PennyWise asks for SMS permission
Reading bank SMS is how the app works — it is the entire product. Everything happens on your device: no server ever receives a message, there is no account to create, and the complete source code is public (AGPL-3.0) so anyone can verify exactly what it does. Would rather not grant it? You can still add transactions by hand or import a PDF statement.

★ Automatic expense tracking
• Every UPI payment, card swipe, ATM withdrawal and bank transfer becomes a categorized transaction
• 143 banks and payment services across 23 countries, with multi-currency support
• Works with any UPI app — it reads your bank's SMS, not the app
• A clean, searchable spending timeline that stays up to date on its own

★ Budgets and insights
• Set monthly budgets per category and watch progress as you spend
• Spending trends, category breakdowns and month-on-month comparisons
• Automatic subscription detection — finds the recurring payments you forgot about
• Smart rules to auto-categorize transactions your way

★ On-device AI assistant
Ask "what did I spend on food last month?" and get a real answer. The AI model (Qwen 2.5) runs locally on your phone — no API calls, no cloud, works offline.

★ Private by design
• No account, no login, no email required
• No ads, no trackers, no analytics on your financial data
• Nothing leaves your device
• Open source (AGPL-3.0) — read the code yourself

★ Free, with an optional Pro upgrade
PennyWise is free to use. Where in-app purchases are available, an optional Pro upgrade adds unlimited custom rules, PDF statement imports, CSV export and duplicate-account merge — and directly supports a solo developer building PennyWise independently. Builds distributed without in-app purchases have all of it unlocked from the start.

A money manager, budget planner and spending tracker in one — for people who hate typing in expenses.
