What's new in 1.1:

• Import slice small finance bank statement PDFs — including multi-page statements, with pot transfers and round-ups tracked as transfers, not spending
• Open any statement PDF with PennyWise straight from Files, Mail, or a share sheet
• New quick-action button on Home: add a transaction or import a statement in one tap
• Choose your display currency in Settings (plus new currencies: ARS, CLP, UYU)
• Smarter Analytics: income vs expenses with a trend against the previous period, and tap any category or merchant to see its transactions
• Cleaner transaction filters — one compact row of dropdown chips
• Accuracy fixes: transfers no longer count as spending in totals or budgets, and Smart Rules now auto-categorize statement imports
